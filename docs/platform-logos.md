# Music service logos

The eight SVG wordmarks in `public/images/platforms/` come from the service artwork used on the label's campaign page, https://srgils.lnk.to/CantLetGo.

Source pattern: `https://services.linkfire.com/logo_{service}_ondark.svg`, using `spotify`, `applemusic`, `youtubemusic`, `tidal`, `amazonmusic`, `pandora`, `qobuz`, and `soundcloud`.

Downloaded September 13, 2026. Preserve the original artwork, proportions, and colors. The assets are stored locally so the listening buttons do not depend on a third-party image request. Accessible link names and direct destinations remain in `SingleLinks` and `lib/release.ts`.
